# Сбер Атлас

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: Next.js, TypeScript, MapLibre, Three.js, local SQLite, Python streaming XLSX import. Initial runtime is local, one operator.

## Users
Relationship managers working with public-sector clients and colleagues preparing regional meetings. A separate public presentation mode supports demonstrations.

## Product Purpose
Move from territory to verified signals, organisations and an editable meeting dossier. Tatarstan is the detailed pilot; Russia is the overview and expansion frame.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Full official municipal hierarchy, honest geometry coverage, accessible buildings and eight authored architectural landmarks. Source-linked news and offices. Local import of the twelve user-supplied XLSX files. FOT in rubles; OD means expected income. Meeting counters are repeated on attachment rows and must be read once per INN and GOSB, with equality validation. Address quality is distinct from bank ownership. Never invent exact pins or territory allocations.

## Brand Commitments
Light architectural scene from the supplied reference, warm stone, pale water, emerald accents, Cyrillic editorial headings and restrained overlays. Desktop and mobile visual sketches precede UI implementation.

## Evidence on Hand
User-provided workbooks remain under Downloads; raw files and imported database are private and excluded from public assets and Git. Official geography and news sources are recorded in the source manifest. Presentation receives only public data.

## Product Principles
Source before inference. Missing is different from zero. Commercial hypotheses remain editable. The map is a working surface; every selected signal can enter a dossier. Mobile controls are designed for touch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard controls, readable contrast, reduced motion, touch targets and a useful list fallback without WebGL.
